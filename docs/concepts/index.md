# Concepts

Ways of seeing a situation: ideas that help you see, frame or tell apart what is happening before you decide what to do. Different from [Patterns](/patterns/) (moves you make), [Reference](/reference/agentic-engineering) (static lookups) and [Skills](/skills/vision) (commands you run).

| Entry | Kind | When it applies |
|:--|:--|:--|
| [Work on the project, in the project](./work-on-and-in) | Context | Using stride for real work surfaces a flaw in stride — the friction is the highest-signal source of what to fix next |
| [Friction is information. The question is which kind.](./friction-distinction) | Distinction | A user hits stride friction — is it signal (push back on the work) or context-mismatch (calibrate the gate)? |
| [The bottleneck moves from the agent to your decisions](./attention-bottleneck) | Concept | Cards build in the background and tweaks land in seconds — what holds you up is now your own decisions, not the agent |
